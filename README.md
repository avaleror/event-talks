## About Me

Cloud-native veteran with 10+ years turning *complex infrastructure* into *clear narratives*. Currently working as Principal Technology Advocate for Edge and Telco at SUSE, with a career spanning Red Hat and Capgemini.

I specialise in making modern infrastructure accessible — whether that means explaining Kubernetes to a room of network engineers, demoing live hardware at a keynote, or writing blog posts that actually get read.

Seasoned speaker at events including KubeCon, Mobile World Congress, SUSECON, OpenNebulaCon, and KCD Spain. Sessions delivered in **English and Spanish**.

**Speaking topics:**

`Kubernetes` `Edge Computing` `Telco & 5G` `IoT` `Arm Architecture` `GitOps` `Cluster Management` `Open Source Infrastructure`
